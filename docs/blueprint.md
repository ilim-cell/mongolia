# **App Name**: Steppe Odyssey

## Core Features:

- AI Personal Concierge: A smart assistant tool that uses reasoning to create a personalized travel plan, selecting destinations like Yolyn Am or the Altai Mountains based on your interest in nomadic culture or extreme adventure.
- Heritage Hub: An interactive library of Mongolian culture, detailing Ger lifestyle statistics, hospitality rituals, and the spiritual history of the Mongol Empire.
- Steppe Map Explorer: A visual navigation system highlighting key landmarks such as Ulaanbaatar and the hidden ice streams of Eagle's Beak Canyon.
- Taste of the Steppe: A curated digital menu of traditional Mongolian foods including Buuz and Airag, complete with their cultural preparation methods and seasonal availability.
- Berkutchi Experience: A focused section on the Golden Eagle hunters, utilizing high-quality media to explain the training process and the bond between hunter and bird.
- Survival & Logistics Guide: Essential climate and gear advice for surviving scorching summers and freezing winters in the Mongolian grasslands.

## Style Guidelines:

- The palette is inspired by the 'Golden Steppe.' The primary color is a rich Ochre Gold (#C39322), reflecting the sun-soaked grasslands and spiritual importance of the sun.
- The background is a soft Sandstone White (#F8F6F1), offering a light and airy canvas that mimics the canvas of a traditional Ger.
- The accent color is a warm Terracotta (#CC4B33), an analogous hue that provides high-contrast call-to-actions while remaining earthy and grounded.
- A sophisticated font pairing of 'Playfair' for headlines, providing a high-end, elegant editorial feel, and 'PT Sans' for body text to ensure warmth and legibility across long heritage descriptions.
- Use minimalist, thin-stroke icons representing nomadic life, such as stylized eagle silhouettes, ger structures, and traditional horses.
- A spacious, asymmetric layout that mirrors the openness of the Mongolian landscape, featuring generous margins and large cinematic imagery.
- Elegant fade-in transitions that mimic the slow sunrise over the steppe to maintain a calm and inviting user experience.
